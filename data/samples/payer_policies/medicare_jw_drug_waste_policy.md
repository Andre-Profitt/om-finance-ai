# Medicare — JW Modifier and Drug Waste Reporting (Illustrative)

Policy: CMS-JW-2026 · Effective 2024-01-01 · Applies to: Medicare Part B claims for single-dose container drugs

## §1 JW Modifier Requirement

Per CMS Change Request and current Medicare Claims Processing Manual guidance, the JW modifier is REQUIRED on all Medicare Part B claims for drugs and biologicals from single-dose containers when the unused amount is discarded after administration. The modifier indicates the discarded amount on a separate claim line that is reimbursable subject to documentation.

## §2 Documentation

The patient's medical record must document the actual amount administered and the discarded amount in the same units billed under the HCPCS J-code. The amount discarded is billed on a separate line with the JW modifier and the corresponding HCPCS J-code.

## §3 Audit Exposure

Claims that bill the full single-dose container amount under the HCPCS J-code without distinguishing the administered amount and the discarded amount, where the JW modifier is required, are subject to audit recovery. Repeated under-reporting of waste is a basis for prepayment review.

## §4 JZ Modifier (No Waste)

Where no discarded amount applies (either because the container is multi-dose, or no unused drug exists), the JZ modifier is appended to attest that no JW-eligible waste occurred for that claim. Claims missing both JW and JZ where required are denied or held for documentation.

## §5 Practice-Level Optimization

Vial-size optimization, dose-banding programs, and waste-reduction initiatives reduce JW-attributed cost to the program while preserving therapeutic intent. Practice-level JW share above benchmarks may be flagged for a vial-management review.
