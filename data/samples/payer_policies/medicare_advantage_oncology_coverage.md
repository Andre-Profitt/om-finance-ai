# Medicare Advantage Plan — Oncology Coverage and Step Therapy (Illustrative)

Policy: MA-ONC-2026-03 · Effective 2026-01-01 · Applies to: Medicare Advantage Part C oncology benefits

## §1 Coverage Aligned to Traditional Medicare

Coverage for infused oncology biologics aligns with the corresponding Medicare LCD plus any plan-specific supplemental coverage and step-therapy requirements permitted under CMS guidance. Plan benefit documents take precedence in case of conflict.

## §2 Step Therapy Under Part B

Per current CMS rules, Medicare Advantage plans may apply step therapy to Part B drugs subject to plan-disclosed criteria and an exception process. Step-therapy denials are denied with CO-197 (precertification/authorization absent) until the appropriate exception or peer-to-peer review resolves the request.

## §3 Prior Authorization

Prior authorization is required for selected high-cost oncology biologics enumerated in the plan formulary. Required documentation includes ICD-10 diagnosis, NCCN compendium recommendation, dose calculation, and prior-therapy attestation when step therapy applies.

## §4 Determinations and Appeals

Standard determinations within 14 calendar days; expedited within 72 hours for urgent cases. Denied claims may be appealed through the Medicare Advantage appeals process: plan reconsideration → independent review entity → ALJ → MAC → judicial review, with timelines per CMS rules.

## §5 NDC-HCPCS Validation

Every J-code claim requires a current-quarter CMS NDC-HCPCS crosswalk match. Mismatches are denied with CO-16; resubmission with the corrected NDC and the original claim reference is required.
