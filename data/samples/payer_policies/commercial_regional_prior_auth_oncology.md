# Commercial Regional Payer Policy — Oncology Prior Authorization

Policy: REG-ONC-2026-14 · Effective 2026-01-01

## §1 Prior Authorization Required

Prior authorization is required for all infused oncology biologics billed under HCPCS Level II J-codes. Claims submitted without a valid PA reference number will be denied with Claim Adjustment Reason Code CO-197 (precertification/authorization absent).

## §2 Formulary Tier and Step Therapy

Reference biologics are placed on Tier 4 (non-preferred). Where a biosimilar is available, the biosimilar is Tier 2 (preferred) and is required as first-line therapy unless medical exception is documented. Failure to follow step therapy will result in CO-197 denial.

## §3 NDC-HCPCS Validation

This plan enforces the CMS NDC-HCPCS crosswalk. Non-matching NDC/HCPCS pairs are denied under CO-16 (coding error). Corrected-claim resubmissions must include the original claim reference and a coding-correction attestation.

## §4 Administration and Billing Site

Site-of-care review applies to agents marked "SOC-managed." These agents must be administered at a Preferred Site of Care (POSC) unless a care-location exception is on file. Non-POSC administration reduces reimbursement to the Preferred-Site allowable and constitutes a contractual underpayment if billed otherwise.
