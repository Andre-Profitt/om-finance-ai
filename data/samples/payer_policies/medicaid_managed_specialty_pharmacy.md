# Medicaid Managed Care Plan — Specialty Oncology Policy

Policy: MMC-SPEC-2026-07 · Effective 2026-01-01

## §1 Covered Drugs

Coverage is limited to the state Medicaid Preferred Drug List (PDL) for oncology biologics and any plan-specific supplement. Non-PDL agents require medical exception authorization; denial reason CO-204 (not covered).

## §2 340B Interaction

For claims submitted by 340B-eligible covered entities, the Medicaid-specific ceiling price applies. Duplicate discount on a 340B-purchased drug (where a manufacturer rebate is also claimed by the state Medicaid agency) is prohibited under 42 U.S.C. § 256b(a)(5)(A). Claims must include the modifier "UD" when the dispensed drug is a 340B-purchased product per state guidance.

## §3 Claim Submission and NDC-HCPCS

All oncology J-code claims must carry an NDC consistent with the CMS NDC-HCPCS crosswalk for the billing quarter. Submission of a mismatched NDC/HCPCS pair triggers CO-16 denial; repeated errors are referred to the plan's program integrity unit.

## §4 Timely Filing and Appeals

Initial claims must be filed within 180 days of service. First-level appeals must be received within 60 days of the denial notice and include documentation of medical necessity, NCCN support, and any required prior authorization reference.
