# Medicare LCD L00000 — Oncology Intravenous Biologic Agents

Policy: L00000 · Effective 2026-01-01 · Jurisdiction: All MACs (illustrative)

## §1 Coverage

Medicare covers infused biologic agents for oncology indications when administered incident to a physician service in a hospital outpatient department (HOPD), provider-based clinic, or freestanding community oncology practice, subject to the requirements below.

## §2 NDC-HCPCS Mapping Requirements

Every claim for a drug billed under an HCPCS J-code MUST include a corresponding National Drug Code (NDC) from the CMS NDC-HCPCS crosswalk for that J-code and effective date. Submission of a valid HCPCS code with an NDC that is not listed in the crosswalk for that HCPCS will result in denial with Claim Adjustment Reason Code CO-16 (claim/service lacks information). Practices must revalidate the crosswalk each quarter.

## §3 J-code Billing and Units

Units billed must reflect the NDC's per-unit dosage as defined by the HCPCS descriptor. Billed-per-unit amounts materially in excess of the published Medicare ASP payment limit (typically above 1.5× ASP+6%) will be reviewed for coding accuracy; routine outliers may trigger prepayment review or downstream chargeback reconciliation.

## §4 Prior Authorization

Unlike many commercial payers, traditional Medicare Part B does not require prior authorization for most incident-to infused biologics. Exceptions include certain high-cost agents identified on the Part B Step Therapy list and specific Local Coverage Determinations.

## §5 Denials and Appeal Process

Denied claims may be appealed through the five-level Medicare appeals process. First-level redetermination requests must be filed within 120 days of the initial determination. Documentation must include the treatment plan, medical necessity justification citing NCCN Compendium or peer-reviewed evidence, and the original claim form with corrections noted.
