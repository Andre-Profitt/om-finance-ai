# Commercial National Payer — Prior Authorization Workflow and Documentation

Policy: ONC-PA-2026-002 · Effective 2026-01-01 · Applies to: All specialty-drug infusions across oncology and multispecialty (retinal, rheumatology, gastroenterology, neurology) covered benefits

## §1 Prior Authorization Requirement

Prior authorization (PA) is required for all infused specialty biologics, including but not limited to oncology immunotherapies, anti-VEGF retinal therapies, IV anti-TNF and IL-23 therapies for rheumatology and gastroenterology, and anti-CD20 therapies for multiple sclerosis. A PA reference number must accompany the first claim in an authorized treatment course. Subsequent claims within the same authorized episode may omit the PA reference if the original reference is retained on file.

## §2 Required Documentation

A complete PA submission must include: ICD-10 diagnosis supporting medical necessity per NCCN, AAO, ACR, ACG, or AAN guidelines as applicable; weight-based or BSA-based dosing calculation; prior therapy history (step therapy) where required; and any payer-specific attestations. Submissions missing required documentation are routed for additional information rather than denied outright; however, the access delay counts against the 14-day determination SLA.

## §3 Expected Determination Timeline

Standard PA determinations are issued within 14 calendar days of a complete submission. Expedited determinations (for urgent-care situations) are issued within 72 hours. Claims submitted before PA determination will be denied with CO-197 until determination is received and the claim is resubmitted with the reference number.

## §4 Access Delay Financial Impact

Each day of access delay between the date of service and authorization determination is tracked as an operational KPI. Network providers with PA delays exceeding the SLA consistently may be flagged for access optimization review, and the financial impact (delayed revenue recognition, patient care impact) is reported at the network level.

## §5 Appeal Path for PA Denials

PA denials may be appealed through peer-to-peer review within 30 days of the denial notice. Appeal submissions must include the original PA submission, additional clinical justification, and if applicable, evidence of medical necessity per the relevant specialty compendium.
