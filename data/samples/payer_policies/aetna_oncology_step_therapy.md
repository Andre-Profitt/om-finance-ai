# Aetna — Oncology Step Therapy and Biosimilar Conversion (Illustrative)

Policy: AETNA-ONC-ST-2026-02 · Effective 2026-01-01

## §1 Step Therapy Sequence

For oncology indications with a payer-preferred biosimilar, the step-therapy sequence requires biosimilar use as first-line within the indicated regimen unless a documented medical exception applies. Documented intolerance, contraindication, or prior trial of biosimilar with inadequate response are accepted exceptions; submissions must include the rationale and supporting clinical notes.

## §2 Conversion Reporting

Network oncology practices report biosimilar conversion rates per HCPCS J-code at the quarterly cadence. Rates below the contracted floor (60% Q4 2026 target) trigger a network-relations review and may affect tiered reimbursement on subsequent quarters.

## §3 Reference-Product Audit

Claims submitted under a reference-biologic NDC when the biosimilar is the payer-preferred agent are subject to an audit comparing billed NDC to the formulary-preferred NDC by quarter. Excess reference-product utilization without an approved exception drives chargeback to the network's specialty distributor.

## §4 Appeal Path

Step-therapy denials may be appealed via peer-to-peer review within 30 days, supported by documentation of the medical-exception rationale per §1.
