# Commercial Payer Policy — Oncology Biologic Coverage (National Plan)

Policy: ONC-2026-001 · Effective 2026-01-01 · Applies to: Fully Insured and ASO groups

## §1 Medical Necessity

Coverage for infused oncology biologics is subject to NCCN Compendium Category 1, 2A, or 2B recommendation for the documented indication. Off-label use without compendium support is not covered and will be denied with Reason Code CO-50 (medical necessity).

## §2 Site of Care

Administration must occur in a provider-based oncology clinic, infusion center, or hospital outpatient department. Home infusion of oncology biologics is generally not covered except under specific case management authorization.

## §3 Approved Indications and Coding

All claims must specify a valid ICD-10 diagnosis consistent with the NCCN-supported indication for the billed J-code. Claims with an ICD-10/J-code mismatch will be denied under CO-50.

## §4 Biosimilar Substitution and Reference Product Requirements

When a reference biologic has an approved biosimilar listed on the formulary, the biosimilar is the preferred agent and must be billed unless an approved medical exception is on file. Submission of a reference-product NDC when the biosimilar is required will result in denial with CO-197 (authorization required) unless a biosimilar-exception prior authorization has been granted.

## §5 Billed Amount Review

Billed-per-unit amounts exceeding 2.0× the current CMS Medicare Part B ASP payment limit will be held for audit. Repeated variance above threshold is a contractual trigger for chargeback reconciliation and may affect network participation status.
