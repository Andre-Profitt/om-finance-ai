# BCBS State Plan — Oncology Prior Authorization Workflow (Illustrative)

Policy: BCBS-ST-ONC-PA-2026 · Effective 2026-01-01 · Applies to: Commercial + Medicare Advantage state plan products

## §1 Prior Authorization Required

Prior authorization is required for all infused oncology biologics, advanced supportive-care agents, and high-cost ancillary therapies. Standard determination is within 14 calendar days; expedited determinations within 72 hours when the prescribing physician certifies clinical urgency.

## §2 Required Documentation

A complete PA submission includes: (a) patient demographics and active coverage information, (b) ICD-10 diagnosis with stage and line-of-therapy notation, (c) weight or BSA-based dosing calculation, (d) NCCN compendium recommendation level (1, 2A, 2B), and (e) prior-therapy summary where step therapy applies. Incomplete submissions are routed for additional information rather than denied; the access-delay clock continues to run during the additional-information period.

## §3 Access-Delay Tracking

Per-claim access delay between the date of service and authorization determination is tracked at the practice level. Practices with sustained delays beyond the 14-day SLA are flagged for an access-optimization review; per-claim financial impact is measured as the operational cost of delayed revenue recognition and patient care friction.

## §4 NDC-HCPCS Coding Validation

This plan enforces the CMS NDC-HCPCS crosswalk on every J-code claim. Non-matching NDC/HCPCS pairs are denied under CO-16. Corrected-claim resubmissions must reference the original claim ID and include an attestation of the coding correction.

## §5 Site of Care

Site-of-care review applies to drugs designated as "site-managed" on the commercial formulary; non-preferred-site administration reduces reimbursement to the preferred-site allowable. Underpayment beyond contractual thresholds is a basis for reconsideration via the contracts team.
