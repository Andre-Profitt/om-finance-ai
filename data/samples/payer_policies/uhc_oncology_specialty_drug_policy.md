# UnitedHealthcare — Oncology Specialty Drug Policy (Illustrative)

Policy: UHC-ONC-SPEC-2026-04 · Effective 2026-04-01 · Applies to: Commercial fully-insured + ASO oncology benefits

## §1 Coverage and Site of Service

Infused oncology biologics are covered when administered in a contracted oncology infusion clinic, hospital outpatient department, or freestanding oncology infusion center. Home administration of complex oncology biologics is generally not in benefit; case-by-case medical exceptions are reviewed against UHC clinical policy bulletins.

## §2 Prior Authorization

Prior authorization is required for all infused oncology biologics billed under HCPCS J-codes. Submissions must include the patient's clinical history, diagnosis ICD-10, weight or BSA-based dose calculation, and prior-therapy attestation when step therapy applies. Determinations: standard within 14 calendar days, expedited within 72 hours. Claims submitted before determination are denied with CO-197 until the PA reference number is attached and the claim resubmitted.

## §3 Biosimilar Tier and Step Therapy

Where the FDA has approved a biosimilar for an oncology reference biologic and the biosimilar is on the UHC commercial formulary, the biosimilar is preferred (Tier 2). Reference-product utilization without an approved biosimilar exception triggers step-therapy denial under CO-197 and is a contractual driver for chargeback reconciliation.

## §4 Coding and NDC Validation

Every J-code claim must carry an NDC consistent with the CMS NDC-HCPCS crosswalk for the date of service. Mismatched NDC/HCPCS pairs are denied with CO-16 and routed to the practice's coding team for corrected resubmission.
