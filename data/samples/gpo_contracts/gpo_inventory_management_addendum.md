# GPO Inventory Management & Waste Reporting Addendum (Illustrative)

Addendum to: GPO-MASTER-2026 · Effective 2026-01-01

## §1 Vial-Size Optimization

The GPO publishes vial-size guidance per HCPCS J-code identifying the lowest-discard vial combination for typical patient weight and BSA distributions. Practice adherence to vial-size guidance reduces JW-attributed waste cost and is reported on the quarterly performance scorecard.

## §2 JW Modifier Compliance

Practices must apply the JW modifier on all claims for single-dose container drugs with discarded amounts per CMS Medicare Claims Processing Manual guidance, and the JZ modifier when no waste applies. JW share materially above the practice cohort median is a flag for vial-size optimization review.

## §3 Inventory Reconciliation

Weekly inventory pulls match dispensed units against billed units across buy-and-bill claims. Variance beyond a 2% tolerance triggers a per-line reconciliation review; recurring overshoot is a basis for an on-site inventory audit.

## §4 Waste Cost Allocation

Waste cost is allocated to the practice's cost-of-goods-sold line for accounting purposes; the GPO's quarterly scorecard reports practice-level waste $ and waste % separately so improvement initiatives can be tracked.

## §5 Drug-Specific Targets

Specific targets and remediation paths apply to high-cost drugs identified in Exhibit D (high $/mg drugs where waste materially impacts P&L). Per-drug targets are reviewed quarterly with the practice's pharmacy operations lead.
