# GPO Payer-Class Differential Pricing Addendum (Illustrative)

Addendum to: GPO-MASTER-2026 · Effective 2026-01-01

## §1 Payer Class Definitions

Eligible payer classes for GPO chargeback are: Medicare Part B (FFS), Medicare Advantage Part C, Commercial National, Commercial Regional, Medicaid Managed Care, Medicaid FFS. 340B-purchased drugs are excluded from GPO rebate accrual and are tracked under the 340B compliance protocol.

## §2 Differential Pricing

Net acquisition price (NAP) is differentiated by payer class for select drugs as enumerated in Exhibit E. Mis-classification of payer class on a chargeback submission results in tier-1 rejection until the dispenser provides corrected eligibility documentation.

## §3 Validation Logic

Each chargeback line is validated against: (a) NDC-HCPCS crosswalk match for the date-of-service quarter, (b) ASP variance threshold per the Chargeback Validation Protocol, (c) payer-class consistency with the contract-qualified dispenser status, (d) 340B exclusion check.

## §4 Audit Trail

The GPO retains audit rights over a rolling 24-month window. Auditable records include payer-class adjudication evidence (payer EOB), NDC-HCPCS reconciliation, and any 340B eligibility determinations relevant to the dispenser status.

## §5 Dispute Resolution

Dispenser disputes are filed within 30 days via the contract-manager portal with required supporting documentation per §3.
