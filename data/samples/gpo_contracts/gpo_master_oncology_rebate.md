# GPO Master Agreement — Oncology Specialty Rebate Schedule

Agreement: GPO-MASTER-2026 · Parties: Network Oncology Practice × National Specialty GPO · Effective 2026-01-01 · Term: 24 months

## §1 Covered Products and Reference Prices

The covered product list includes all oncology infused biologics and their biosimilars as enumerated in Exhibit A. Reference prices for each covered product are set at the CMS Medicare Part B ASP payment limit published for the billing quarter. The payment limit in effect at the date of service controls for rebate accrual purposes.

## §2 Tier Definitions

| Tier | Quarterly Volume Threshold    | Applies To           |
| ---- | ----------------------------- | -------------------- |
| T1   | 0–50,000 billable units       | All covered products |
| T2   | 50,001–150,000 billable units | All covered products |
| T3   | 150,001+ billable units       | All covered products |

Volume is aggregated at the network level, not the practice level, and computed quarterly in arrears.

## §3 Rebate Percentages

Rebates are paid on net allowed revenue for covered products dispensed within the quarter and NOT 340B-purchased or Medicaid duplicate-discount-flagged. Percentages:

- T1: 2.0% of net allowed
- T2: 3.5% of net allowed
- T3: 5.0% of net allowed

Biosimilar-conversion incentive applies per §6 of the Biosimilar Conversion addendum and is additive up to a combined cap of 7.0% of net allowed.

## §4 Eligibility

Practices must be in good standing (no material breach, no network-participation suspension) and must submit quarterly utilization data through the GPO portal within 30 days of quarter close. Late submission forfeits the quarter's rebate.

## §5 Exclusions and Exceptions

The following are excluded from rebate accrual: (a) claims on 340B-purchased product, (b) compassionate-use or clinical-trial claims, (c) claims reversed or denied by the payer, (d) claims with material coding errors including NDC-HCPCS mismatches or billed amounts more than 1.5× ASP for the billing quarter.
