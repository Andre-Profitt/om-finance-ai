# GPO Specialty Drug Distribution Addendum (Illustrative)

Addendum to: GPO-MASTER-2026 · Effective 2026-01-01

## §1 Scope

Specialty distribution covers oncology + multispecialty injectable biologics (retinal anti-VEGF, rheumatology IV anti-TNF/IL-23, gastroenterology biologic therapy, neurology anti-CD20). Distribution channel: contracted specialty distributor identified in Exhibit C. Wholesale acquisition cost (WAC) and contracted net acquisition price (NAP) tables are refreshed quarterly.

## §2 Buy-and-Bill Workflow

For practices billing under buy-and-bill (practice purchases the drug and bills the payer post-administration), eligible chargeback occurs at point of dispense with the contracted NAP applied. The practice is responsible for inventory management and waste tracking per Medicare JW guidance.

## §3 White-Bag and Brown-Bag Exclusions

Drugs received via payer-mandated white-bag (specialty pharmacy ships to practice for administration) or brown-bag (patient brings drug to administration) channels are NOT eligible for buy-and-bill chargeback. Practices report these claims separately for inventory reconciliation and rebate-exclusion accrual.

## §4 Inventory Reconciliation

Quarterly inventory true-up matches dispensed units against billed units; variance beyond a 2% tolerance triggers an inventory audit and may reduce rebate accrual for that quarter.

## §5 Substitution Rules

Substitution of a non-contracted product without contractual exception is a chargeback-validity failure under §3 of the Chargeback Validation Protocol; the substituted claim is rejected and the dispenser may be subject to make-whole on the price differential.
