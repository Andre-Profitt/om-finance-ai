# GPO Biosimilar Conversion Addendum

Addendum to: GPO-MASTER-2026 · Effective 2026-01-01

## §1 Purpose

Aligns practice prescribing with biosimilar substitution targets to accrue incremental rebate and maintain preferred-pricing access on reference biologics.

## §2 Reference and Biosimilar Pairs

Reference products and their qualifying biosimilars are enumerated in Exhibit B. For purposes of this addendum, conversion is measured as:

    conversion_rate = units_biosimilar / (units_biosimilar + units_reference)

at the HCPCS J-code level, aggregated quarterly.

## §3 Conversion Targets and Incentives

| Quarterly Conversion | Incremental Rebate |
| -------------------- | ------------------ |
| 0–40%                | 0.0%               |
| 41–70%               | 1.0%               |
| 71–90%               | 2.0%               |
| 91–100%              | 2.5%               |

Incremental rebate is additive to base tier rebate, capped at 7.0% combined under §3 of the Master Agreement.

## §4 Data Reporting

Practices must submit HCPCS- and NDC-level volume to the GPO portal. Submissions that do not distinguish reference NDCs from biosimilar NDCs (per the CMS NDC-HCPCS crosswalk) are ineligible for the incremental rebate and may trigger chargeback validation review.

## §5 Reference-Product Audit

Claims billed under a reference-product NDC when the payer has designated the biosimilar as preferred (see payer formulary policies) are subject to chargeback reconciliation. The audit compares billed NDC vs. payer-preferred NDC by billing quarter; excess reference-product utilization reduces the effective rebate.
