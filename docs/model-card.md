Following the model-card structure defined in `docs/governance.md` §5 (SOX change control) and §10 (TPM artifacts).

## 1. Intended use

Score every rule-flagged exception candidate in the O&M Finance AI Control Tower with a calibrated probability of recoverable revenue leakage. The calibrated score, multiplied by `dollars_at_risk`, ranks the reviewer queue.

**Out of intended use:**

- Scoring clinical outcomes or patient risk
- Auto-decisioning any payer submission, appeal, or chargeback
- Scoring claims that did not pass rule-based candidacy filters (the candidate universe is the input, not the full claim population)

## 2. Model architecture

- Family: gradient-boosted trees (LightGBM binary classifier)
- Objective: `binary`, metric `auc`
- Training config (v1): 300 boosting rounds max with early-stopping at 25; `num_leaves=31`; `min_data_in_leaf=20`; `learning_rate=0.05`; `feature_fraction=0.9`; `bagging_fraction=0.9`; `bagging_freq=5`
- Seed: 20260424 (deterministic per-session runs; production re-trains weekly)

## 4. Training data (v1 — synthetic)

- 5,000 synthetic oncology + multispecialty claims generated by `oaifinance.ingest.synthetic_claims`
- 18 HCPCS total: 10 oncology + 4 retinal/rheum/GI/neuro + 4 second-line oncology/multispecialty additions; 4 payer archetypes; 20 practices across 5 specialties
- Drug prices anchored to real CMS ASP Part B sample
- Latent `is_true_error` state observed noisily by rules (no label leakage)
- `_true_leakage` label defined as `recoverable_dollars > $200`
- 75/25 stratified train/valid split on the flagged-exception subset

**Production transition:** V2 replaces synthetic labels with retrospective appeal-outcome + re-adjudication labels pulled from the practice billing system under DUA.

## 5. Performance

**Current numbers live in [`docs/current-results.md`](current-results.md)** and are regenerated by `make demo`. This card describes what the model is and what its acceptance bars are; it does NOT pin specific run numbers (they drift with seed, corpus, and exception-type changes).

**Acceptance bars:**

| Metric                                                  | Acceptance bar                          |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Valid AUC                                               | ≥ 0.85 (debug-only; never the headline) |
| Calibrated precision @ top-100                          | ≥ 0.85                                  |
| Calibration slope (5-fold CV isotonic, sample-weighted) | within [0.85, 1.15]                     |
| Citation precision on explained exceptions              | ≥ 0.90                                  |
| Abstention rate                                         | within [10%, 40%]                       |

Live numbers from the latest run are written to `artifacts/eval_report.json` and to MLflow per training run.

**Known limitations:**

- Labels are synthetic ground truth; production behavior will differ.
- Calibration is fit out-of-fold via 5-fold CV on the current synthetic candidate universe; production requires rolling-window validation on retrospective labels.
- Imbalance: the candidate universe has a ~53% base rate (vs. ~7% in the full claim population); the model is scoring candidates, not classifying raw claims.
- Access-PA features are new in v1; expected to tighten as the PA corpus expands in V2.

## 6. Calibration

- Method: `sklearn.isotonic.IsotonicRegression` with `out_of_bounds='clip'`, fit out-of-fold across a 5-fold StratifiedKFold (with single-holdout fallback when the positive class is too small to stratify)
- Slope measurement: sample-count-weighted least-squares fit through bin-centered (predicted, observed) points (see DL-0015)
- Output: `risk_score_calibrated` column stored alongside raw `risk_score`
- Monitored: calibration slope per run; alarm threshold outside [0.85, 1.15] for two consecutive runs → model-refresh ticket

## 7. Explainability

Two layers:

1. **Model-intrinsic:** gain-based feature importance per training run, logged to MLflow
2. **Per-exception:** upstream RAG explainer quotes a cited policy / contract clause for every explained exception; abstains when retrieval is weak. See `docs/governance.md` §8 for the citation-contract design.

## 8. Bias + fairness considerations

- No patient-level attributes used as features. No race, sex, age, or geography.
- Practice-level attributes (`is_340b_practice`, specialty) are included for product-correctness reasons (e.g., 340B compliance scenario requires the flag). These are not demographic proxies.
- Specialty one-hots could produce uneven performance across specialty groups if training data is dominated by one specialty; v1 eval does not yet report per-specialty precision, but v2 slicing is scheduled.

## 9. Governance
